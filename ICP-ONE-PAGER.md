## Natural partner (Keep / deepen)

Warm · relational · balances sharp business *and* relationships · craft over hustle · curious about space/feeling or practice · open to co-host or refer · calm confidence · “let’s try a small thing” · mutual upside · already gathers people.

**Known anchors:** Paz / Resonance Collingwood (Dissolve space); Isabel (HeartMind friend-partner); Carol (past collab).

---

## Hard sell / Hard Pass

**Hard Pass:** extractive · one-way only · disrespectful to craft or people · flaky / late-pay · pressure-close · bait-and-switch · cheapest/fastest only · needs a huge crew you don’t have · hostile · asks you to hide who you are · volume mill with no care for space or person.

**Soft Pass / Later (not automatic no):** purely material worldview · very corporate · “prove ROI” first · not wellness-curious · slow to warm · not a network fit *yet*. Materialism alone ≠ hard Pass.

---

## Capacity & quality bar (HeartMind)

Care how space feels — not production / cheap-as-possible painting. Small/medium jobs you can carry without a huge team (more work later can grow capacity). Paint as decorating a home or hosting space (classes, merchandise, living), not “slap paint to sell.”

## What partnership celebrates (why)

- **Merit-profit:** value, uplift, hope, bridges, supporting lives — not only money  
- **Financial abundance** including **non-linear prosperity** (not only trading hours)  
- **Networks:** you need access now; later you connect others both ways  
- **Practice lineage:** martial arts, yoga, meditation — useful from healing (−10) to growth (+10)

## Briefing checklist (for research bots)

For each company + key people, answer:

1. What do they do, for whom, where?  
2. Public values / how they talk about space, craft, people, or practice?  
3. HeartMind fit? Dissolve fit? Both / neither? Why.  
4. Natural partner vs hard sell vs soft Pass signals (use words above).  
5. Sources for every fact; blank if unknown — never invent email/phone.  
6. Suggested first offer + one careful angle (draft only; human Keep before tone).
